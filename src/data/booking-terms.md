These terms apply to every booking of billysroom ("the DJ", "I", "me") by a promoter, venue, brand or private client ("you"). They sit alongside the confirmation email for your event, which sets out the date, venue, set time, set length and fee. Where the two differ, the confirmation email wins.

## 1. confirming a booking

- A date is only confirmed once you have accepted the confirmation email **and** paid the deposit.
- The deposit is **70% of the fee**, unless otherwise agreed in writing (for example, a 50% deposit).
- Dates are not held without a deposit. A date offered to you is held for **3 days**; after that it may go to another booking.
- Paying the deposit means you have read and accepted these terms.

## 2. fees and payment

- The fee is the amount stated in the confirmation email.
- The deposit (**70%**, unless otherwise agreed) is due to confirm the booking.
- The **remaining balance is due 24–48 hours before the event**, and in all cases no later than 24 hours before the set starts. A reminder will be sent ahead of the due date.
- Payment is by bank transfer to the account given in the confirmation email. Bookings outside Nigeria are invoiced in the currency agreed in writing.
- Any transfer or bank charges are covered by you, so the full fee is received.
- If the balance has not been received 24 hours before the set, I am not obliged to perform. This is treated as a cancellation by you (section 3), and the deposit is kept.

## 3. if you cancel or postpone

Cancellations must be made in writing to dj@billysroom.today. Notice is counted from the day the email is received to the event date.

| notice given | what happens |
|---|---|
| **30 days or more** | Deposit refunded, minus a **10% admin fee** |
| **14–29 days** | Deposit is non-refundable |
| **Less than 14 days** | The **full fee** is due, including the remaining balance |

**Postponing instead of cancelling:** if you give at least 14 days' notice, you can move the booking **once** to a new date within **6 months**, subject to my availability. Your deposit carries over to the new date. If the new date is later cancelled, the cancellation terms above apply from the original deposit.

Refunds are paid within **14 days** of the written cancellation.

## 4. if I cancel

- If I have to cancel for any reason within my control, or because of illness or emergency, **everything you have paid is refunded in full** within 7 days, or moved to a new date if you prefer.
- Where possible, I will give as much notice as I can and recommend a replacement DJ of a similar style. Any arrangement with a replacement DJ is between you and them.
- My liability is limited to refunding the amounts you have paid me.

## 5. events outside anyone's control

If the event cannot go ahead because of something neither of us can reasonably control (for example severe weather, government restrictions, civil unrest, venue closure or a major transport or power failure), neither of us is in breach. We will first try to move the booking to a new date within 6 months, with your deposit carried over. If no new date can be agreed, payments are refunded minus any costs already incurred and non-refundable (such as travel already booked).

## 6. set times and performance

- Set time and length are agreed in writing before the event.
- I aim to arrive at least **30 minutes** before my set.
- If my set starts late because of the event running behind, the agreed end time stays the same unless we agree otherwise on the night.
- Playing beyond the agreed set length is at my discretion and may be charged at an agreed hourly rate, payable on the night.
- Music selection is at my discretion within the style agreed for the booking. Reasonable requests are welcome but not guaranteed.

## 7. equipment and tech rider

You provide, set up and pay for professional, working DJ equipment in line with my tech rider, including as a minimum:

- 2 or more **Pioneer CDJs** (CDJ-3000 or CDJ-2000NXS2) with USB, and a **Pioneer DJM mixer** (DJM-900NXS2, DJM-A9 or equivalent)
- a **booth monitor** I can control, separate from the main sound
- a sturdy, level table at a comfortable height, with stable power
- cover from rain and direct sun for any outdoor booth

Equipment should be set up, connected and tested before my set starts. If equipment is missing or faulty and is not fixed within a reasonable time, I may shorten or stop the set. This does not entitle you to a refund. You are responsible for any damage to my personal equipment caused by the venue, staff or guests.

## 8. safety and conduct

- The booth should be kept clear of anyone not involved in the set.
- You are responsible for providing a safe working environment. If I feel unsafe, or I am harassed, I may stop performing and leave, and the full fee remains due.

## 9. changes to these terms

Any change to a booking must be agreed in writing (email or WhatsApp message counts). These terms may be updated from time to time; the version on this page when your deposit is paid is the one that applies to your booking.

Questions? Email **dj@billysroom.today**.
